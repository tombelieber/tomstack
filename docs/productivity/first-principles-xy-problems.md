# First Principles and XY Problems

## What it does

Separates the desired outcome from a proposed mechanism, inspects relevant
reality, and produces a decision-ready recommendation before implementation.

## When to reach for it

Invoke `$first-principles-xy-problems` for ambiguous product, business,
strategy, architecture, or workflow decisions that need deep discovery.

## Common questions

- Will it implement the recommendation? No. It is deliberately design-only.
- Is every proposed feature treated as wrong? No. It is treated as a hypothesis
  that must connect to the outcome.
- Does deep mean long? Only material uncertainty is expanded.

## It's working if

Verified facts, assumptions, unknowns, alternatives, and the remaining decision
are distinguishable, and implementation has not started prematurely.
